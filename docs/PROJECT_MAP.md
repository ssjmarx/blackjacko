## src/core/card.ts

Card encoding for both 52-card and 48-card (Spanish) decks; byte = rankIdx * 4 + suit.
rankIdx: 0=A, 1..8 = 2..9, 9='10', 10=J, 11=Q, 12=K; suit: 0=spades, 1=hearts, 2=diamonds, 3=clubs.
Spanish decks omit rankIdx 9 (the '10'), giving the 48-card shoe.

- `makeCard(rankIdx: number, suit: number)` — Pack a rank and suit into the one-byte card encoding.
- `cardRankIdx(card: number)` — The rank index (0=A .. 12=K) of a card byte.
- `cardSuit(card: number)` — The suit (0=spades, 1=hearts, 2=diamonds, 3=clubs) of a card byte.
- `cardValue(card: number)` — Blackjack value of a card: ace = 11 (soft, reduced where needed), faces/10 = 10.
- `cardFaceValue(card: number)` — Value for strategy keys: face cards collapse to 10, ace stays 11.
- `isTenValue(card: number)` — True for any 10-valued card (10/J/Q/K) — the Hi-Lo −1 class.
- `isAce(card: number)` — True when the card byte is an ace (rankIdx 0).
- `cardName(card: number)` — Human-readable short name, e.g. "AS" or "TH" (rank letter then suit letter).
- `upcardValue(card: number)` — Upcard value used as strategy table key: 2..9, 10, 11(ace).
- `rankCount(spanish: boolean)` — Number of distinct ranks in this game's deck (13 standard, 12 Spanish).
- `deckSize(spanish: boolean)` — Deck size in cards.
- `buildDeck(decks: number, spanish: boolean)` — Build the full ordered card list for a shoe of `decks` decks (Spanish skips the '10' rank).

## src/core/count.ts

CountEngine (S3, S12, Errata E1). Hi-Lo (52c): A −1 · 2-6 +1 · 7-9 0 · 10v −1.
Monkey (48c, balanced): A −2 · 2-6 +1 · 7-9 0 · J/Q/K −1. TC = RC ÷ max(decks remaining, 0.5), floored (Schlesinger).
Knowledge rule: counts anything seen or quantified — the hole card only at reveal; CSM recycle un-sees discards.

- **class CountEngine** — Running/true count over one ruleset's shoe; exposure events drive it, not deals.
  - `reset()` — Zero rc and the seen count (fresh shoe).
  - `expose(...cards: number[])` — A card (or cards) becomes visible / quantified.
  - `knownRemoval(...cards: number[])` — Known removal from play (Hot Hand / Cold Deck): face-down out-of-play counts as seen (S12).
  - `recycle(...cards: number[])` — CSM recycle: discards return to unknown — their tags are subtracted.
  - `runningCount()` — The raw running count.
  - `cardsSeen()` — Cards exposed (or quantified) so far.
  - `decksRemaining()` — Decks remaining by seen-count (never negative).
  - `trueCount()` — Floored true count (default convention, user-locked).
  - `rawTrueCount()` — Unfloored (raw) true count — used for Kelly sizing and diagnostics.
- `tagTable(system: 'hilo' | 'monkey')` — The per-rank tag table for a count system (index = rankIdx, 13 entries).

## src/core/exact.ts

Exact finite-deck EV engine (Braun-style): memoized dealer distributions keyed by
composition hash, stand/hit EVs, US peek conditioning (natural mass removed, renormalized).
Purpose: audit/derive Spanish 21 strategy cells with no published transcription (spec E4).

- **class ExactEngine** — Exact-EV probe over a mutable rank composition (counts[rankIdx] = cards remaining).
  - `remaining()` — Total cards left in the composition.
  - `remove(card: number)` — remove a seen card from the composition
  - `restore(card: number)` — Undo a remove() — put a seen card back into the composition.
  - `compHash()` — The composition hash — the dealer memo key (base-97 packed rank counts).
  - `rankValue(r: number)` — value of a rank in blackjack terms
  - `dealerDist(upVal: number)` — dealer outcome distribution; the up card must be removed from counts
  - `standEv(playerTotal: number, upVal: number)` — stand EV for player total vs conditioned dist (stake 1)
  - `dealerRec(total: number, soft: boolean)` — Dealer outcome dist (IDX_* indices) continuing from (total, soft); memoized by composition.
- `freshCounts(decks: number, spanish: boolean)` — A fresh full-shoe rank composition for `decks` decks (Spanish omits the '10' rank).
- `upval(card: number)` — face value used for strategy tables: 2..9, 10, 11(A)

## src/core/hand.ts

Hand evaluation: totals, naturals, and Spanish 21 bonus detection.
Pure functions over card-byte arrays; the resolver and stats consume them.
Bonus voiding after double/split is the caller's job (ruleset flags).

- `handTotal(cards: number[])` — Best blackjack total (aces degrade 11→1 as needed) plus whether it is soft.
- `isNatural(cards: number[])` — True for a 2-card 21 (blackjack / natural).
- `isBust(cards: number[])` — True when the best total exceeds 21.
- `isSplitPair(cards: number[])` — True when two cards share the same face value (split eligibility, 10s mix allowed).
- `sameRank(a: number, b: number)` — True when two cards share the same rank (used for Sp21 suited-7 checks and stats).
- `sp21TripleBonusPossible(cards: number[])` — Can this 2-card hand still complete a 3-card 6-7-8 or 7-7-7 bonus? (Sp21 chart '*' modifier)
- `sp21BonusMultiplier(cards: number[])` — Sp21 bonus multiplier on a 21 (3c 678/777: 1.5 mixed / 2 suited / 3 spades; 5c 1.5, 6c 2, 7c+ 3), else 0.
- `isSuitedTripleSeven(cards: number[])` — Suited 7-7-7 in exactly 3 cards (super bonus precondition).

## src/core/player.ts

I18 deviations > unconditional LS overlay > WoO base chart; Sp21: count-in-hand chart + rescue (E4).
Betting flat / TC ramp / Kelly; insurance never or I18 #1 (floored TC >= +3); Sp21 insurance never.

- **class SimPlayer** — The Strategy implementation driving the sims: bet sizing, insurance, and per-line play decisions.
  - `betUnits(tc: number | null)` — Bet size for the next round, decided pre-deal from the current TC.
  - `insurance(v: InsuranceView)` — Insurance decision from the I18 #1 index (never on Spanish 21, E4).
  - `play(v: PlayerView)` — The action for the active line: rescue → Sp21 → Fab 4 → I18 → LS overlay → base chart.

## src/core/resolver.ts

HandResolver (S12, stage-1 scope: no promo hooks — dispatch points marked `// PROMO:`).
Round: deal+expose → insurance (ace up) → US peek → player turn (hit/stand/double/split/
LS/rescue, resplitMax, aces per ruleset) → dealer turn → settle; the hole counts only at reveal (S3).

- `newTally()` — A zeroed RoundTally for optional diagnostics collection.
- `newLine(stake: number)` — A fresh play-phase line carrying one stake.
- `playRound(env: RoundEnv)` — Play one full round to settlement; net/initialWager/atRisk are in units. Awaits promise-returning strategies at every decision point.
- `canSplit(line: Line, lines: Line[], rules: Ruleset)` — Split legality: pair, not doubled, under resplitMax, aces honor rsa on a resplit.
- `splitLine(line: Line, lines: Line[], betUnits: number)` — Pop the pair's second card into a child line; both lines carry fromSplit/splitAces.
- `doubleOnOk(rules: Ruleset, ht: { total: number; soft: boolean })` — Double-range legality ('any' | '9-11' | '10-11'; restricted ranges are hard totals only).
- `buildView(line: Line, lines: Line[], up: number, upVal: number, rules: Ruleset, shoe: Shoe)` — Assemble the PlayerView for a line, computing every legality flag and the live count values.
- `clampAction(action: Action, view: PlayerView)` — Force an illegal action to its standard fallback (D→H, P→S, R→H, RESCUE→S).

## src/core/shoe.ts

Shoe (S12 MODULE Shoe): queue + cut card + known-removal + CSM recycle.
Dealing does NOT update the count — exposure events do; the caller (HandResolver)
owns when cards become "seen" (the hole card stays uncounted until revealed).

- **class Shoe** — The dealt shoe: Fisher-Yates queue, cut card, count engine, CSM recycle; testing helpers included.
  - `shuffle()` — Fisher-Yates over the full shoe; resets count to fresh-shoe state.
  - `deal()` — Pop the next card (reshuffles first if a deep-split round exhausts the shoe — ~1e-8/round).
  - `dealSpecific(card: number)` — Deal a specific card (testing): swaps the wanted card to the front.
  - `dealRank(rankIdx: number)` — Deal the next card of a given rank (testing only).
  - `dealTenValue()` — Deal the next ten-value card (testing only).
  - `remaining()` — Cards left in the live queue (total minus dead minus dealt).
  - `dealtCount()` — Cards dealt so far (penetration diagnostics).
  - `needsShuffle()` — True when a new round must trigger a shuffle first (cut card is out).
  - `discard(card: number)` — Stage a finished round's card for the CSM (already counted at exposure).
  - `removeKnown(n: number, klass: 'low' | 'high', windowSize = 20)` — Known removal (S12): pull `n` low/high cards from the next `windowSize`, face-down out of play (stage-3 promos).
  - `recycleDiscards()` — CSM recycle (S12): discards rejoin the queue and become unknown again.

## src/sim/runner.ts

Headless simulation runner: N rounds of one scenario, streamed stats.

## src/sim/stats.ts

Streaming statistics for simulation rounds (units-based): mean, variance,
standard error, EV conventions (initial-bet and element-of-risk), and the
bankroll curve (peak / max drawdown).

- **class Stats** — Incremental round tally — O(1) per round, closed-form reports.
  - `addRound(net: number, initialWager: number, atRisk: number)` — Fold one settled round into the accumulators.
  - `mean()` — Mean net per round (units).
  - `variance()` — Population variance of round net (floored at 0).
  - `sd()` — Standard deviation of round net.
  - `seMean()` — Standard error of the mean.
  - `evPctInitial()` — classic house-edge-style EV: net / initial main bets
  - `seEvPctInitial()` — approx standard error of evPctInitial (delta method)
  - `evPctAtRisk()` — element-of-risk style EV: net / all money at risk
  - `unitsPer100()` — Units won per 100 hands.
  - `seUnitsPer100()` — Standard error of unitsPer100.
  - `avgInitialBet()` — Mean initial main bet (units).
  - `handsPerShoe()` — Hands per shoe (0 when no shoe boundary was counted).
- `fmt(n: number, digits = 3)` — Fixed-digits format; normalizes negative zero; '–' for non-finite.
- `pct(n: number, digits = 3)` — Fraction to a percent string.

## src/ui/cardstack.ts

The count-true card visuals (spec E11/E14): a deck hangs from its shoe cell
and bleeds down into deck extra; a shoe plants at its cell's window-side
edge and runs toward the pit — stuck in place, dealt sprites vanishing.

- `fitFootprint(…)` — The footprint law (spec E13/E14): the first card centers in its cell at card size; the back yields to the run; the clamp is the last resort. Horizontal rows plant at the window-side edge and grow toward the pit.
- `buildStack(…)` — Build the stack: the full deck drawn once at fixed positions, dealt sprites hidden from the dealt end.
- `deckStack(…)` — Create the vertical deck stack: card-sized in its shoe cell, bleeding down, the top card on top.
- `shoeRow(…)` — Create the horizontal shoe row: planted at the window-side edge, running toward the pit, the tail planted.

- `influence(k: KnotSpot)` — A knot's influence radius — where its swirl stops shaping the field.
- `seedKnots(outer: Rect, rng: ()` — Seed 6-12 knots window-wide, rejection-sampled so none crowd each other.

- `largestBlankCells(outer: Rect, spots: KnotSpot[])` — The largest blank square's side, in occupancy cells — the wall-to-wall law's measure.
- `largestBlank(outer: Rect, spots: KnotSpot[])` — The largest region farther than every knot's influence, or null when coverage holds.

- `paintWood(…)` — Paint the flow-field wood across the whole window; the caller insets the felt on top.
