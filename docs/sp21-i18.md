# docs/sp21-i18.md — Spanish 21 Illustrious 18 + Fab 4 (human transcription)

**Status: NEEDS VERIFICATION.** These indexes are the human's transcription
from their own card-counter notes. Source: Katarina Walker's *The Pro's Guide
to Spanish 21 and Australian Pontoon*, adjusted **+4** to match the balanced
Monkey count used by the game. The indexes are specifically for **8-deck
games with redoubling** — the only ruleset the human has personally seen in
person. They have been simmed in Casino Verite and should improve EV, but
they may not be the most perfect they can be: perfecting them (spec E4's
empirical-derivation offer) is a possible future research task.

**Conventions:**

- Every line carries an implicit **"and above"** — the action applies at TC ≥
  the index.
- **X** = any 10-value card (J/Q/K — there are no tens in a Spanish deck).
- **FORFEIT A DD** = double-down rescue (forfeit the doubled hand, S10).

**The 17 v A play** (correct — ploppies HATE it): Spanish 21 players should
generally surrender 17 vs A, but in the rare case you arrive at a hard 17 vs
A *after hitting* — surrender no longer available — stand only at +2 and
above, and hit otherwise. That is the "STAND 17 VS A AT 2" row below.
